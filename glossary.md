# Glossary - Tenkuu no Escaflowne (The Vision of Escaflowne)

Binding for every translator and reviewer. Agents propose additions in reports; only the controller edits this file.
`tools/batches.py check` enforces the JP -> EN columns and the Avoid column. Name tags come from `tbl/names.tsv`.

## Rulings (asked of the user once; options and reasons in docs/decisions.md)
- Honorifics: kept, hyphenated (Van-sama, Hitomi-san, Boris-dono, senpai). Name order: Western (given name first).
- Romanisation source: official English names (Bandai Entertainment anime release) where they exist, else an earlier fan patch (none exists), else Hepburn without macrons. Katakana game-original names that plainly transcribe a foreign word use that word.
- Credits: English.
- Register: the anime's: serious fantasy drama, modern plain speech, slightly formal for knights and courtiers. Sound effects and cries: spelled as English interjections, not romaji.
- Abbreviations: full names and terms everywhere, prefer engine changes over shortening.

## Style rules
- Punctuation map: 「 opens spoken lines, close it at the end of the entry: "..."; a line with no 「 is a thought or aside: no quotes. 『』 -> '...', ・・・ and … -> ..., ！？ -> ?!
- Thoughts: no quotes. Emphasis: capitals, sparingly.
- Voices: `docs/style-reference.md`.
- 運命: "fate", always. "Destiny" only inside the two Engine names and the Engine's call 運命改変！ ("Destiny, altered!!").
- 想い: "will" where it is the Atlantis power (人の想い, 想いの力), "wish" where the zone grants it (想いをかなえる), "feelings" between people.
- 引力: "attraction" (Dornkirk's and Folken's theme); "gravity" only for the physics in syo05_032.
- 兄上 / お兄様 / 父上 / 母上: "Brother", "Father", "Mother" as forms of address.

## Decisions from earlier batches
- Tarot: Hitomi's card names keep their Italian forms (Il Pazzo, La Giustizia, L'Imperatore); the game's suits are Dragons, Birds, Fishes, Beasts.
- Merle speaks of herself as "Merle" where the JP has メルル.
- A title that doubles a -sama ("His Majesty Van Fanel-sama") is cut to the -sama form.
