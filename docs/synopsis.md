# Synopsis (from the extracted script; the game retells the anime in compressed form)

Chapters are the overlays `SYO01`-`SYO07`. Read before translating or reviewing: it holds what one batch can't show.

## Who is who
- **Hitomi Kanzaki:** high-school runner and tarot reader from Earth (the Mystic Moon to Gaeans). Her visions grow; later learned to pull futures toward her fears.
- **Van Fanel:** young King of Fanelia, part Draconian (hidden white wings), bound to Escaflowne by blood: its wounds are his.
- **Allen Schezar:** Knight Caeli of Asturia. **Secretly Prince Chid's father** by Marlene. His sister was stolen ten years ago.
- **Merle:** catgirl raised with Van. **Millerna:** Asturia's third princess, trained in medicine, loves Allen, engaged to Dryden.
- **Dryden Fassa:** rich merchant-scholar, Millerna's fiance, pays for Escaflowne's repair.
- **Folken:** Van's elder brother, believed dead, Zaibach's strategist. Defects in ch7, dies in Dornkirk's reaction.
- **Dornkirk:** Emperor of Zaibach. **Is Isaac, a scientist from Earth** (Isaac Newton in all but name). Built the Destiny Alteration Engine.
- **Dilandau:** Dragonslayers' captain. **Is Allen's lost sister Celena**, remade by Zaibach's sorcerers (reveal ch7): text says "he" until then, "she" from Allen's line syo07_290 on.
- **Naria and Eriya:** catgirl sisters loyal to Folken, die as fortune-enhanced soldiers (ch6).
- **Refine, Mizuru:** the game's own characters: a Zaibach pilot devoted to Dilandau, and Hitomi's friend pulled in and controlled (ch5).

## Chapters
1. Hitomi is pulled to Gaea with Van after his dragon hunt. Zaibach burns Fanelia; Balgus dies. Allen's fort falls. Van is captured and meets Folken; Hitomi proves her sight with tarot and finds him.
2. Palas: Millerna; Folken at King Aston's court; the arena; Dilandau burns the city. Escaflowne's energist throws them to Earth and back. Dragons' graveyard; Van's wings. Allen is wounded; Millerna operates.
3. Freid: Prince Chid; Zongi the shapeshifter murders Plakt, poses as him and frames Allen; Hitomi nearly dies under hypnosis and Van revives her. Millerna and Hitomi learn who fathered Chid. The Duke dies; Chid surrenders the Sword of the Seal.
4. Van's body mirrors Escaflowne; Ispano repair it on Dryden's money. Van goes berserk, Hitomi and Merle pull him back. Leon's journal leads to the Mystic Valley: Atlantis, Gaea created by will, Hitomi's own anxiety choosing bad futures. Grandma's "believe".
5. Dornkirk summons them and tells his story. They escape; Mizuru. Dryden and Millerna to marry. Operation Golden Rule of Love: Folken and Eriya play Allen and Hitomi to bend her heart toward Allen.
6. Hitomi rigs Millerna's reading, the wedding is attacked at an eclipse, she gives herself up. Naria, Hitomi's offer, Naria's name. Naria and Eriya die of the backlash. Van and Hitomi at Fanelia; land dragons; Folken turns.
7. Folken defects; Allen proposes; Dilandau breaks down. Hitomi flees to Earth, Van follows, Celena surfaces. War; Folken dies at Dornkirk's hands; the zone of absolute fortune makes everyone's wish fight everyone's. Hitomi lets go of her fear: Celena returns, the Engine fails.

## Who knows what
- Chid's parentage: Hitomi (vision, ch3) and Millerna; Allen confesses to Hitomi in ch7.
- Dornkirk's identity: Hitomi guesses it in ch5 (syo05_024).
- Dilandau is Celena: no one before syo07_139; don't foreshadow in English beyond the JP.
